# IRIS paper archive

This folder contains the two current paper deliverables and the local assets needed to reproduce their PDF compilations.

## IRIS paper

- IRIS paper/iris-paper.tex is the primary full paper source. It is the current main-paper version previously stored as publication/iris/iris-synopsis-draft.tex.
- IRIS paper/iris-paper.pdf is its fresh compilation.
- IRIS paper/iris-detailed-draft.tex is the longer mathematical companion draft.
- IRIS paper/IRIS_RESEARCH_PAPER_BLUEPRINT.md is the planning and structure document.
- IRIS paper/figures/ contains the application, guide, and verification figures referenced by the LaTeX sources.

## IRIS synopsis

- IRIS synopsis/synopsis-paper.tex is the current six-page synopsis source.
- IRIS synopsis/synopsis-paper.pdf is its fresh compilation.
- IRIS synopsis/section-word-counts.md records the section structure and approximate word counts.
- IRIS synopsis/figures/ contains the two figures used by the synopsis.

All copied LaTeX sources use local figures/ paths, so the archive does not depend on the original workspace locations for its included figures.
